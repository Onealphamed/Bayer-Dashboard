Follow-up sequence

Send these only if there's no reply. Stop the moment someone answers. Each follow-up replies on the same thread.

Who gets what

Wave 1 is CXOs, VPs, heads and senior managers with a verified company email. Wave 2 is brand and product managers. Hold covers anyone with no email, an email on another company's domain, or a note that they may have moved.
